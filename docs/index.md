# Welcome to IrregularChat

The **IrregularChat** Community of Interest (COI) is where tech enthusiasts, researchers, and innovators converge. This wiki is a dynamic space to discuss the latest in technology, share knowledge, and collaborate on projects.

*Embrace a spirit of professional impatience and be ready to break down silos!*

## Rules of Engagement

For the full list and community FAQ see the [IrregularChat Forum](https://forum.irregularchat.com/t/irregularchat-community-faq-guidelines/4).

1. Leave your rank and ego at the door.
2. Stay on topic to maintain focus. Mark jokes and sarcasm with `/j` or `/s`. Do NOT joke about leaking or spilling classified information. Others will report this action to their S/G/J2.
3. Avoid sharing confidential, personally identifiable information (PII) or classified information. Encryption does not make it permissible.
   1. Navigate content using the appropriate levels and direct users to relevant content locations.
4. Chatham House Rule used throughout, unless stated otherwise. Participants can use information shared but must not reveal the source or identity of the speaker outside of the IrregularChat Community.
5. Don't be disrespectful to members.
6. Engage with the Wiki and Forum:
   1. Utilize the wiki for resources. If you find gaps, fill them in and share them in the chat.
   2. Notice inaccuracies or missing information? Lead the effort to correct and enhance it.
   3. If you encounter a missing page, take the initiative to create it.

## Sharing Content

### Before Posting

1. **Search first** - Check the wiki and [Forum](https://forum.irregularchat.com) before sharing
2. **Add context** - Share context, not just links
3. **Use tags** - Use #tagged categories for organization
4. **Scan files** - Use [VirusTotal](https://www.virustotal.com/gui/home/upload) for any downloads
