### Harness Single Point Release and MOLLE Setup

1. **Turn Over the Adjustable Cross Strap:**

### Flip the **adjustable cross strap** on the **Harness Single Point Release (HSPR)** to access the D-ring attachment points.

### Pass the **release handle cable** through the release handle cross strap and secure it using hook pile tape.

1. **Attach the Triangle Links:**

### Feed the **triangle links** through the **white attaching loop** on the harness, ensuring that the snap hooks face downward.

### Thread the **green attaching loop** through the white attaching loop and secure it.

### Pass the **red attaching loop** through the grommet on the cross strap, ensuring it is aligned correctly.

1. **Route the Release Handle Cable:**

### Run the **release handle cable** through the **red attaching loop** for additional security.

### Guide the cable through the **webbing retainer** and the **cable loop retainer** to keep the cable organized and free of tangles.

1. **Prepare the Harness:**

### Lay the harness down with all components facing downward, ensuring proper layout for the following steps.

1. **Position the MOLLE:**

### Place the **MOLLE** face down on the harness, with the adjustable shoulder straps facing upwards. The **E-tool** or **canteen pouch** should fit within the cross strap of the MOLLE.

1. **Attach the Equipment Retainer Strap:**

### Run the **equipment retainer strap** through the shoulder holes of the MOLLE, ensuring it runs on the outside of the shoulder straps.

### The straps should be routed diagonally towards the bottom of the MOLLE, below the shoulder straps.

### Feed the **friction adapter** through the bottom of the MOLLE, below the plastic frame, running it towards the shoulder straps.

### Connect the equipment retainer strap to the opposite-side friction adapter. Secure and tighten it with a quick release, pulling the assembly upwards towards the shoulder straps.

1. **Manage Excess Straps:**

1. **S-roll** any excess straps, ensuring no more than three fingers' worth of excess remains. Secure the rolled-up straps with rubber bands to prevent them from hanging loosely.

1. **Attach the Lowering Line:**

### Take the **looped end hook pile tape lowering line** and run it from the shoulder area down towards the bottom of the MOLLE.

### Ensure the lowering line passes under the MOLLE and loops back onto itself, pulling it up at the intersection of the equipment retainer straps for secure attachment.

### Steps for MAWC (Modular Airborne Weapons Case) Rigging

1. **Nose Cone Adjustment:**

### Ensure the **adjustable nose cone** is sized correctly for an M4 dummy rifle with the butt stock fully collapsed.

### Tighten the **nose cone securing straps** around the rifle to prevent movement.

1. **Snap Shackle Preparation:**

### Adjust the **snap shackle** to the 4th rung of the **Pouch Attachment Ladder System (PALS)** on the MAWC.

### Ensure the snap shackle's **locking pin** is securely engaged, and the **yellow safety lanyard** is correctly attached and stowed in the PALS webbing.

1. **Compression Straps:**

### Fully extend the **compression straps** and quick-release buckles on the MAWC.

### After positioning the weapon, tighten the compression straps, ensuring that the excess webbing is secured with the webbing retainers.

1. **Slide Fastener and Tabbed Thong:**

### Zip up the **slide fastener** and close the **tabbed thong**, ensuring the slide fastener is secured to the first rivet pin, but not so far as to engage the spring mechanism.

### Ensure the **snap fastener** is correctly closed.

1. **Upper Tie-Down Tape:**

### Route the **upper tie-down tape** through the opening on the tabbed thong, securing it with a single or double-looped bow knot on the lead edge of the MAWC.

1. **Final Adjustments:**

### Confirm that the weapon is properly oriented, all adjustable buckles are secured, and the compression straps are tight. Ensure the snap shackle and all fasteners are engaged.
