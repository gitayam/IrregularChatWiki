### Introduction
This guide helps users employ AI/ML technologies effectively to enhance research collection, processing, and analysis. It provides structured prompts with placeholders for direct use in conversational AI applications like ChatGPT.

### Prompting Strategies

### 1. Write Clear Instructions
Ensure your instructions are specific and direct to get the most relevant and accurate responses from AI.


#### Prompt Template:
```
&quot;Analyze [detailed description of the topic or data](ml](/ml)) focusing on [aspects](specific). Summarize the findings in [length or format](desired).&quot;
```

### 2. Provide Reference Text
Giving reference texts helps the AI understand the context or background of your query better.


#### Prompt Template:
```
&quot;Considering the information provided in [text or document](reference), discuss [question or topic](specific). Include any necessary citations from [sources or databases](specific).&quot;
```

### 3. Split Complex Tasks
Complex tasks should be divided into simpler, more manageable sub-tasks.


#### Prompt Template:
```
&quot;Step 1: Outline the main arguments found in [or text](document). Step 2: Evaluate the implications of these arguments on [field or issue](specific). Step 3: Predict future trends based on this evaluation.&quot;
```

### 4. Give the Model Time to “Think”
Prompt the AI to consider the response more deeply by asking it to explain its reasoning process.


#### Prompt Template:
```
&quot;Before answering, consider the factors affecting [or issue](topic). What are the potential impacts of [factor](specific)? Explain your reasoning.&quot;
```

### 5. Use External Tools
Leverage the AI’s ability to integrate with external tools for enhanced data retrieval or analysis.


#### Prompt Template:
```
&quot;Use the data from [database or API](external) to calculate [calculation](specific). Summarize the results and explain how they impact [topic or issue](related).&quot;
```

### 6. Test Changes Systematically
Regularly evaluate the effectiveness of your prompts and the accuracy of the AI’s responses.

