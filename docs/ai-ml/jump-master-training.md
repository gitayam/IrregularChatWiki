### Airborne Study

### Five Points of Performance
Remember with: Every Cat Loves Perfect Landings.

1. (E) Proper Exit, Check Body Position, and Count

### After exiting the aircraft, get into a tight body position and count to 4

1. (Canopy) Check Canopy and Gain Canopy Control

### Reach up, grab the risers, and do a full 360 degree check of your canopy.

1. (Lookout) Keep a Lookout for Other Jumpers and Release Equipment

### Maintain separation from fellow jumpers and release any equipment.

1. (Prepare to Land) Prepare to Land with a Parachute Landing Fall (PLF)

### Get into position for a proper PLF, focusing on hitting the five points of contact.

1. (Land) Land and Execute a PLF

### Upon landing, conduct a proper PLF by hitting all five points (balls of feet, calf, thigh, buttocks, and the pull-up muscle).
