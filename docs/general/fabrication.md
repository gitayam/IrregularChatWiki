# Fabrication

**Manufacturing** is producing goods using equipment, labor, machines, tools, and chemical or biological processing or formulation. It is the essence of the secondary sector of the economy. [- Fabrication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/wikipedia_manufacturing)

For community knowledge management, fabrication, and rapid prototyping are used to plan, establish, use, and counter fabrication for unique training and operational problem sets.
