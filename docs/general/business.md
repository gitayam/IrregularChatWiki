# Business

## The Business Chat Page
Welcome to the Civilian-Military Business Chat Archive. This section is a comprehensive resource for veterans and civilians looking to explore business insights, connect with others, and access valuable resources. Navigate through our curated discussions and resources to enhance your business acumen and networks.

A sanctuary for military personnel venturing into business, addressing the distinct divide many feel in traditional workplaces, and fostering a community bound by collective experience, intelligence, and ambition.

This chat prioritizes mentorship, financial strategies, and opportunities to redefine career narratives and empower members to carve out prosperous, harmonized futures in both domains.

### Programs Highlight
Highlighting specific programs that are tailored to veterans and their unique business needs:

- **Pentagon Federal Credit Union Foundation’s VEIP Program**: Offers financial assistance and mentorship to veteran entrepreneurs.

- **Bunker Labs CEO Circle**: A networking group providing support and guidance from established veteran CEOs.

- **NYU Tandon’s Apex Program**: Focuses on advanced technological support and business incubation for veteran-led startups.

### Additional Resources
Explore more resources to further support your business endeavors:

- **[Veteran Entrepreneurship Training Series](https://vets.sba.gov/)** - A series of free courses aimed at helping veterans start and run successful businesses.

- **[VetFran Business Grant Program](https://www.vetfran.org/)** - Grants and franchise opportunities for veterans interested in starting their own business.
