## Military Funding Programs

### Credentialing Assistance (CA)

- **Amount**: $4,000/year
- **Purpose**: Professional certifications aligned with career goals
- **Portal**: [ArmyIgnitED](https://www.armyignited.com/app/)
- **Guide**: [Credentialing Assistance](/general/credentialing-assistance)

### SOF Education Program (SEP)

- **Amount**: Up to $10,000/year
- **Purpose**: Individual knowledge gaps in special operations roles
- **Separate from**: TA and CA (additional funding)
- **Requirements**: SF-182, no planned separation within 18 months
- **Guide**: [SEP Funds Guide](https://u.pcloud.link/publink/show?code=XZ0frk0ZchOJr4ELEVkiFyB7Ic45CmhSpaEk)

### Tuition Assistance (TA)

- **Amount**: Varies by service
- **Purpose**: Degree programs and education
- **Note**: Separate from CA funding

## CompTIA Token Process

1. Complete training on [Army eLearning](https://usarmy.percipio.com/)
2. Navigate to ACTMS website
3. Upload cyber awareness certificates
4. Submit token request (bottom of page)
5. Receive voucher code for exam

**Note**: Processing can take up to 6 months - plan accordingly.
