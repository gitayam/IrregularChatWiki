# Quadrant Crunching

**What It Is:** An imaginative thinking technique that uses a 2x2 matrix to explore alternative scenarios and generate new insights.

**When to Use It:** For long-range forecasting or when the future is particularly uncertain.

**Step-by-Step Guide:**
1. **STEP 1**: Break the lead hypothesis into component parts and identify critical dimensions. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 354)
2. **STEP 2**: Use 2x2 matrices to generate alternative scenarios. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 354)
