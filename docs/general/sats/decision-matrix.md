# Decision Matrix

**What It Is:** A diagnostic technique for systematically comparing multiple options against a set of criteria.

**When to Use It:** When a decision needs to be made from a list of options based on a clear set of criteria.

**Step-by-Step Guide:**
1. **STEP 1**: Identify the decision or question. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 454)
2. **STEP 2**: List criteria and options. Consolidate items to remove overlap. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 454)
3. **STEP 3**: Assign weights and score options. Calculate the total score and choose the best option. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 456)
