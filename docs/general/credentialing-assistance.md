# Credentialing Assistance

## What is Credentialing Assistance (CA)
Credentialing Assistance (CA) is a program designed to help service members obtain professional certifications and credentials that align with their career goals, enhancing their skills and employability.

## What is SANS
SANS (SysAdmin, Audit, Network, and Security) Institute is a trusted organization that provides advanced training and certifications in cybersecurity, equipping professionals with practical skills to protect and defend networks and systems.

## Recommended Certification using Credentialing Assistance (CA)
Check out the recommended [SANS Roadmap](https://www.sans.org/cyber-security-skills-roadmap/).

[Cybersecurity Skills Roadmap](#)

Recommended Certification: [SEC504: Hacker Tools, Techniques, and Incident Handling: GIAC Certified Incident Handler (GCIH)](https://www.sans.org/cyber-security-courses/hacker-techniques-incident-handling/).

### Use TA for SANS Credentialing Assistance (CA)

- Create a SANS Account at [SANS Account Creation](https://www.sans.org/account/create/) using your .mil email.

- Order On-Demand SANS Training at [SANS On-Demand](https://www.sans.org/ondemand/).
  - Select your desired training and proceed to checkout.
    - Recommendation: [GIAC Certified Incident Handler (GCIH)](https://www.sans.org/cyber-security-courses/hacker-techniques-incident-handling/).
  - Select “Pay by Phone or Multiple Credit Cards.”
  - Note your order number.

- Email for further assistance and discount details:
  ```

I request to have the discount down to $4k (the total annual amount for CA) for the following training:

*Email being Forwarded to SANS*

```

- Complete the ArmyIgnitED portion after receiving the updated SANS quote:
  - Log in to [ArmyIgnitED](https://www.armyignited.com/app/).
  - Request Credentialing Assistance at [Credentialing Assistance Request](https://www.armyignited.com/app/student/credential-paths/request).
    - Select “Other for MOS.”
    - Search for Pathway: GIAC Certified Incident Handler (GCIH) if using the above recommendation.
    - Upload the quote received from the SANS email.
  - Profit!
