# Archival-research

## Tools and Methods for Archival Research
Return to [Section](/research/) See [Leveraging historical research methods enriches OSINT investigations. This guide outlines tools and archival resources, as well as methodological insights for OSINT practitioners.

The effectiveness of archival research in OSINT lies in your ability to creatively dig deeper, build personal collections, and critically assess the context and content of your findings.

### Embracing Historical Methods in OSINT

- **Underutilized Archives**: Explore lesser-known archives for unique finds.

- ' [https://researchworks.oclc.org/archivegrid/ Archive Grid (US)](/general/notes)

- ' [Archives Hub (UK)](https://archiveshub.jisc.ac.uk/)

- [WorldCat](https://www.worldcat.org/)

- **Libraries as Resource Hubs**: University and public libraries can be a goldmine for research.

- ' [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/)

- ' [National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/)

### Building Personal Research Archives

- **Digital Archiving**: Creating personal archives is vital for storing and indexing sources.
  - **[HTTrack](http://www.httrack.com)**: Website copier for offline browsing.
  - **Hunchly**: Tool for capturing online research sessions.
**' **[Zotero](/research/research-citations)**: Reference manager for saving and organizing research materials.

### Critical Analysis of Sources

- **Reading Against the Grain**: Interpret sources critically, looking for what’s omitted and exploring different perspectives.
  - **Lumen Database**: Tracks copyright takedown requests.
  - **Multilingual Wikipedia**: Check entries in other languages for different content.

* **Talk Pages**: Provides context and debates behind Wikipedia content.

### Tools for Archival Recovery

- **Recovering Deleted Content**: Tools and methods to uncover what’s been deliberately removed or lost.
  - **Google Transparency Report**: Shows removed content from search results.
  - **Wayback Machine**: Access archived versions of web pages.

* **DocFetcher**: Desktop search application for creating a searchable index.

### Contextualizing and Managing Research

- **Contextual Understanding**: Grasping the socio-cultural backdrop of the sources to comprehend their full implications.
  - **Forensic OSINT**: Investigate using digital forensic principles.
  - **OSINT Framework''': Collection of OSINT tools and resources.
  - **Deleted Tweets**: Services to find removed Twitter content.
