## Rules of Engagement

:::note[Community Standards]
1. **Leave rank and ego at the door** — We're all here to learn
2. **Stay on topic** — Mark jokes with `/j` or `/s`
3. **No classified or PII** — Encryption doesn't make it okay
4. **Chatham House Rule** — What's said here stays here
5. **Be respectful** — Disagree professionally
6. **Contribute back** — Fill gaps, fix errors, create content
:::

For the full community FAQ, see the [Forum Guidelines](https://forum.irregularchat.com/t/irregularchat-community-faq-guidelines/4).

:::tip[Before Sharing Content]
**Search first** — Check the wiki and forum before posting duplicates.
**Add context** — Share insights, not just links.
**Scan files** — Use [VirusTotal](https://www.virustotal.com/gui/home/upload) for downloads.
:::
