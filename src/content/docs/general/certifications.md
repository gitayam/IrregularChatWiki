## Tips

Using educational resources from [Hack The Box Academy](https://academy.hackthebox.com/) helps prepare for intermediate cybersecurity certifications like the OSCP. Registering with a school email reduces the monthly cost to **$8/month** (student plan), covering all Tier I-II modules including the full CPTS path.
