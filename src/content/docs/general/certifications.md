Certification pathways by domain with resources, costs, and military funding options. See [Certification Funding](/military/certification-funding) for how to pay for these.

:::tip[Military Funding]
- **Army CA**: $2,000/year ([details](/military/credentialing-assistance))
- **GI Bill**: Up to $2,000/exam reimbursement
- **COOL**: Free vouchers by MOS at [cool.osd.mil](https://www.cool.osd.mil/)
- **Free programs**: Microsoft MSSA, AWS re/Start, Google Career Certs, Salesforce Military
- Full guide: [Certification Funding](/military/certification-funding)
:::

:::tip[HTB Academy Student Discount]
[Hack The Box Academy](https://academy.hackthebox.com/) costs **$8/month** with a school email (Tier II access), covering the full CPTS certification path. See [OSCP page](/cybersecurity/oscp) for details.
:::

## Cybersecurity

**SANS/GIAC**
- [GPEN — Penetration Tester](/cybersecurity/gpen)
- [GWAPT — Web Application Penetration Tester](/general/gwapt)
- [SANS Roadmap](https://www.sans.org/cyber-security-skills-roadmap/) — career progression

**Offensive Security (OffSec)**
- [OSCP — Penetration Testing with Kali Linux](/cybersecurity/oscp) — includes HTB/THM prep guide
- [OSWE — Advanced Web Attacks](/general/advanced-web-attacks-and-exploitation-oswe)

**CompTIA**
- [CASP+ (CAS-004)](/general/casp)
- **Security+** — DoD 8570 baseline. Study: [Jason Dion practice exams](https://www.udemy.com/user/jason-dion-2/) and [Professor Messer](https://www.professormesser.com/) (free). Don't pay $1,200 for the bundle — buy voucher separately via [Pearson VUE Gov Store](https://govstore.pearsonvue.com/shop/comptia) or use [COOL](/military/certification-funding).

**CMMC (Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification)**
- Required for DoD contractors — [SBA CMMC Roundtable info](https://advocacy.sba.gov/2026/02/24/department-of-war-cybersecurity-maturity-model-certification-cmmc-program-small-business-impacts-roundtable/)
- Small business cost estimates: higher than initially projected
- Relevant for anyone starting a defense contracting company

**Free**
- [ISC2 CC](https://www.isc2.org/Certifications/CC) — free entry-level security certification

## Project Management

Detailed guide: [Project Management Certifications](/general/project-management-certifications)

- **PMP** — $405 (PMI member). PMP holders earn 29-33% more. Military experience qualifies.
- **CAPM** — $225 entry-level alternative (no experience required)
- **PSM I** — $150 Scrum certification (best value Agile cert)
- **SAFe Agilist** — ~$895 (most common enterprise Agile)
- DAU courses count toward PMP education requirements

## Drone / UAS

Detailed guide: [FAA Part 107](/general/faa-part-107)

- **Part 107 Remote Pilot** — $175 exam, no flight hours required
- Part 108 (BVLOS) framework expected 2026 — new premium tier
- Part 107 + clearance + GIS = $90,000-$130,000

## Aviation

Detailed guide: [Aviation Certifications](/general/aviation-certifications)

- **PPL** — $15,000-$30,000 (not GI Bill eligible on its own)
- **Military pilots**: Skip checkride via Part 61.73 + Military Competency test
- **R-ATP**: Military pilots qualify at 750 hours (vs 1,500 standard)
- [RTAG](https://www.rtag.org/) — nonprofit for veteran aviation careers

## Career Networking

:::tip[Join Professional Associations Early]
Start going to local meetups and join relevant professional associations **before** you transition. For infosec/cyber: ISSA, ISACA, local DefCon group, hacker meetups. You don't need all of them — find the one most active in your area. These connections are what get you interviews, not just the cert.
:::

**Community insight**: 15 years of cyber experience without formal certs makes the job market brutal. Get at least one industry cert to get past HR filters, even if your skills are solid.

**Active job postings shared by community members (2026):**
- ARSOF remote cyber TMs (Maryland) — seeking OSCP, OSWP, OSED, PEN 100/200
- AI SOC Engineer at Tyto Athene (Reston, VA) — SOCOM-focused
- IT Manager at MetroStar (Rosslyn, VA) — CISSP + TS/SCI
