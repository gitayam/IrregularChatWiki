# Computer Network Operations (CNO)

**Computer Network Operations (CNO)** encompasses offensive and defensive cyber operations as defined by the **U.S. Department of Defense (DoD)**. It includes three primary areas of focus:

- **Computer Network Attack (CNA)**: Offensive operations to disrupt or disable adversary networks.

- **Computer Network Defense (CND)**: Measures to protect and secure critical systems.

- **Computer Network Exploitation (CNE)**: Intelligence-gathering activities within adversary networks.

This page provides an overview of CNO, its pathways, prerequisites like the **Computer Network Assessment Battery (CNAB)**, and additional resources to aid in preparation and career advancement.

### Pathway to CNO Careers

### Step 1: Passing the CNAB
The **Computer Network Assessment Battery (CNAB)** is a prerequisite evaluation for many CNO-related roles. It assesses foundational technical skills and cognitive abilities, ensuring readiness for advanced training. Key components include:

- **Computer Networking Test**: Focus on networking fundamentals like IP addressing and routing.

- **Matrix Test**: Evaluates pattern recognition and abstract reasoning.

- **Work Background and Preferences Assessment**: Gathers professional and technical experience relevant to CNO roles.

### Step 2: Cyber Operations Training
After passing the CNAB, candidates undergo comprehensive training that covers:

- **Network Security and Defense**: Principles of securing and monitoring networks.

- **Offensive Operations**: Techniques in penetration testing, vulnerability exploitation, and malware deployment.

- **Advanced Tools**: Hands-on experience with industry tools and frameworks.

### Step 3: Certification
Candidates often pursue certifications that validate their technical skills and knowledge:

- **Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH)**

- **Offensive Security Certified Professional (OSCP)**

- **GIAC Penetration Tester (GPEN)**

- **Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP)**

### Step 4: Assignment to Specialized Units
Graduates of CNO programs are typically assigned to specialized cyber units such as:

- **NSA Cyber Operations**

- **U.S. Cyber Command**

- **Military Branch Cyber Teams** (e.g., Army Cyber, Air Force Cyber Warfare Operator, Navy CWE)

### Resources for CNAB and CNO Preparation

### Study Resources

- **Networking Fundamentals**: O'Reilly CCNA Books and Cisco Networking Academy resources.

- **Pattern Recognition Practice**: Online cognitive and abstract reasoning tests.

- **Programming & Scripting**: Learn Python, Bash, and PowerShell with free resources like Codecademy and ShellSpells.

- **Cybersecurity Basics**: Explore courses from platforms like Coursera, edX, and Cybrary.
