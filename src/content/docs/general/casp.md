# Casp

## CASP+ (CompTIA Advanced Security Practitioner)
Return to the [Page](/general/certifications)

CASP+ (CompTIA Advanced Security Practitioner) is an advanced-level cybersecurity certification designed for security architects and senior security engineers responsible for leading and improving an enterprise's cybersecurity readiness. It validates advanced-level competency in risk management, enterprise security operations and architecture, research and collaboration, and enterprise security integration.

### Overview
CASP+ prepares professionals to:

- Lead cybersecurity initiatives across enterprise environments.

- Design and implement secure solutions tailored to complex challenges.

- Manage advanced security operations and risk mitigation strategies.

### Why Pursue CASP+?

- **Advanced Knowledge**: Covers technical skills and knowledge to design and engineer secure solutions.

- **Leadership Skills**: Prepares professionals to lead security teams and projects.

- **Vendor-Neutral**: Applicable across a wide range of technologies and methodologies.

- **Industry Recognition**: Globally recognized certification enhances career opportunities.

### Exam Details

- **Exam Code**: CAS-004

- **Format**: Multiple choice and performance-based questions

- **Number of Questions**: Maximum of 90

- **Duration**: 165 minutes

- **Passing Criteria**: Pass/Fail (no scaled score provided)

- **Prerequisites**: Recommended: 10 years of IT experience, including 5 years in security.

[Official CompTIA CASP+ Page](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/casp)

### Key Domains

### 1. Security Architecture

- Enterprise security expansion

- Cloud and virtualization technologies

- Cryptographic techniques and application

- Integration of network and security components

### 2. Security Operations

- Managing and implementing security solutions

- Advanced incident response techniques

- Risk management processes

- Compliance and governance frameworks

### 3. Risk Management

- Identifying and managing risks

- Implementing risk mitigation strategies

- Conducting risk assessments

- Business continuity planning

### 4. Research and Collaboration

- Interpreting trend data and anticipating cyber defense needs

- Collaboration with industry experts and stakeholders

- Applying research to enhance security solutions

### 5. Integration of Enterprise Security

- Integrating security across diverse environments

- Implementing secure systems in hybrid, on-premises, and cloud environments

- Balancing security with business needs
