  <TabItem label="Aviation">
    Detailed guide: [Aviation Certifications](/general/aviation-certifications)

    - **PPL** — $15,000-$30,000 (not GI Bill eligible on its own)
    - **Military pilots**: Skip checkride via Part 61.73 + Military Competency test
    - **R-ATP**: Military pilots qualify at 750 hours (vs 1,500 standard)
    - [RTAG](https://www.rtag.org/) — nonprofit for veteran aviation careers

    :::caution[GI Bill and Flight Training]
    PPL alone is NOT GI Bill eligible. GI Bill covers instrument, commercial, CFI, and above — must attend a VA-approved Part 141/142 school.

## MOS-to-Certification Quick Reference

| MOS/Rate | Recommended Cert Path | Target Salary |
|---|---|---|
| 25-series (Signal) | Net+/Sec+ → [Cloud certs](/general/cloud-certifications) | $90k-$150k |
| 35-series (Intel) | [Part 107](/general/faa-part-107) + GIS + analytics | $75k-$120k |
| 17-series (Cyber) | [OSCP](/cybersecurity/oscp) / CISSP / cloud security | $100k-$160k |
| 15-series (Aviation) | [Part 107](/general/faa-part-107), A&P, [PPL](/general/aviation-certifications) | $75k-$130k |
| 12-series (Engineer) | [PMP](/general/project-management-certifications) + [trade certs](/general/trade-certifications) | $80k-$140k |
| 11-series (Infantry) | [PMP](/general/project-management-certifications) + Agile + clearance | $80k-$130k |
| Navy IT/CT | CompTIA trifecta → [cloud](/general/cloud-certifications) | $90k-$150k |
| Air Force 1D7/3D | [AWS + Azure](/general/cloud-certifications) + clearance | $100k-$160k |

Look up your specific MOS at [Army COOL](https://www.cool.osd.mil/army/), [Navy COOL](https://www.cool.osd.mil/usn/), [Marine COOL](https://www.cool.osd.mil/usmc/), or [Air Force COOL](https://www.cool.osd.mil/usaf/).

## Career Networking

:::tip[Join Professional Associations Early]
Start going to local meetups and join relevant professional associations **before** you transition. For infosec/cyber: ISSA, ISACA, local DefCon group, hacker meetups. You don't need all of them — find the one most active in your area. These connections are what get you interviews, not just the cert.
:::

**Community insight**: 15 years of cyber experience without formal certs makes the job market brutal. Get at least one industry cert to get past HR filters, even if your skills are solid.

**Active job postings shared by community members (2026):**
- ARSOF remote cyber TMs (Maryland) — seeking OSCP, OSWP, OSED, PEN 100/200
- AI SOC Engineer at Tyto Athene (Reston, VA) — SOCOM-focused
- IT Manager at MetroStar (Rosslyn, VA) — CISSP + TS/SCI
