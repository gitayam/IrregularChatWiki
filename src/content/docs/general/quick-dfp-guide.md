## Privacy & Security Tools

### Pre-Browsing Checks

Check to confirm your configuration and VPN:

1. [DNS / IP Leak Test](https://dnsleaktest.com/) - Confirm your location is what you want it to be
2. [Cover Your Tracks (EFF)](https://coveryourtracks.eff.org/) - Check browser fingerprinting
3. [Browser Cookie Tester](https://www.grc.com/cookies/forensics.html)
4. [InAppBrowser](https://inappbrowser.com/)
5. [AdBlock Test](https://canyoublockit.com/extreme-test/) - Verify your adblocker or DNS is blocking ads

### Password Manager

See the [Password Manager Section](/general/guide-to-password-managers).

- **KeePass** ([iOS](https://keepassium.com/) | [Android](https://www.keepassdx.com/) | [Windows, Mac, Linux](https://keepassxc.org/download/) | [Web (Works on NIPR)](https://app.keeweb.info/))

#### Password Managers to Avoid

- LastPass

### Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

- [Cheapest 2FA YubiKey](https://www.yubico.com/product/security-key-c-nfc-by-yubico/) (Hardware U2F is BEST)
- **2FAS** ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/2fas-auth/id1217793794) | [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.twofasapp) | [Chrome Extension](https://2fas.com/browser-extension/))
- **OTP Auth** ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/otp-auth/id659877384))
- **Aegis** ([Android](https://getaegis.app/))
- **Authenticator** ([Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/auth-helper/))

#### Authenticators to Avoid

- **SMS** - Vulnerable to SIM-jacking attacks
- **Authy** - Requires phone number, not open-source
- **Google Authenticator** - Sync is not E2EE

### VPN

Full VPN Guide: [VPN Recommendation](/privacy/vpn-recommendation)

Top VPNs for privacy and security:

1. **[MullvadVPN](https://mullvad.net/en/)**
   - No email association
   - Proven privacy record
   - Accepts cash/crypto
   - .onion available

2. **[iVPN](https://ivpn.net)**
   - No logging
   - Transparent privacy policy
   - Supports multiple protocols

3. **[ProtonVPN](https://protonvpn.com)**
   - Proven privacy record
   - Free tier available
   - Works with OpenVPN config files

#### VPNs to Avoid

- ExpressVPN
- PIA VPN
- CyberGhost
- Zenmate
