# Series Packet

## Enclosure 3 Review Workflow
**Purpose**: To reduce variance, improve efficiency, expand continuity and interoperability, increase transparency, and allow for systemic adjustments to the review.

### Broad Steps

- Ensure the Task is in the OPCEN RFS Tracker.

- Upload Enclosure to SIPR Portal.
  - Name file: *SERIES NAME_ENC 3_Date_Changes (EX. SERIES NAME_ENC 3_202406_Changes)*.

- Review past guidance.

- Contact with Team (Initial).

- Read Enclosure.

- Review and validate with documented changes:
  - Ensure MO, SMO, DB, and admin data are identical across enclosures.
  - Confirm that Refined Assessment Criteria are a logical derivative of Initial Assessment Criteria.
  - Look for proof of research or verification if a dollar amount or cost is provided.

- Contact with Team as needed with RFIs.

- Confirm Formatting.

- Apply corrections.

- Submit for review:
  - OPCEN Leadership.
  - Company Leadership.

- Leadership will return to the team if needed (ENC 3 may not require a return to the team; bring in the team on substantial changes).

### Steps Taken for Enclosure 3 Review

- **Ensure that task is in the RFS Tracker**: [OPCEN RFS](https://socom.sharepoint-mil.us/:l:/s/usasoc-1sfc-4pog-1bn-c/FKFS5PZyXDlAqKWw8vtI16MB8QlVLYOcNUeHJVbj2U27eg?e=Thht51).
  - If the task is not in the RFS Tracker, add it and appropriately include the requester's email and team.
  - When it is on the RFS tracker, assign yourself and change the status of the RFS to "IN PROGRESS" as you work on it.

- Upload Enclosure to SIPR Portal.
  - Create a folder in the Country Directory:
    - Directory should be: In Development-COUNTRY-SERIES-01. Series Enclosures-03. Enclosure 3.
  - Name file: *SERIES NAME_ENC 3_Date_Changes (EX. SERIES NAME_ENC 3_202406_Changes)*.
  - Add link to working directory in country chatsurfer (SIPR).
  - Leave the original copy in the appropriate enclosure folder and create a "changes" version in the working folder.

- **Review past guidance** from the J39 about Enclosure 3.
  - Review PSYOP Kneeboard **[Series Overview]**: (SIPR): `https://YOURTSOC.smil.mil/sites/Ops/JED/J39/Kneeboards/`
    - Find the most current.
    - Review the intent of series groups.
  - Review Objectives and Effects (SIPR): `https://YOURTSOC.smil.mil/sites/j5/SOFAssessments/`
  - Ensure portion markings are not overclassified or missing.

- **Contact team for initial comms**.
  - Send Email to PSY-T, CC PSY-D Leadership, and FUOPS Leadership.
  - The goal is to set up a communication channel for future discussion and to clarify who is working on the RFS and who is the customer of the RFS.

- **Read** the entire Enclosure 3.
  - Ensure ENC 3 information still matches ENC 1 and 2.
  - Review header slide for correct series name and team information.
