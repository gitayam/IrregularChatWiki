## PMP (Project Management Professional)

### Requirements

| Education | PM Experience Required | Hours Leading Projects |
|---|---|---|
| 4-year degree | 36 months | 4,500 hours |
| HS diploma or associate | 60 months | 7,500 hours |

Plus 35 hours of PM education (courses, training, DAU).

:::tip[Military Experience Counts]
PMI defines projects as "temporary efforts creating unique products/services/results." Most military missions qualify: deployment coordination, logistics operations, training events, leader assignments, family readiness programs. See [PMI's Military Guide (PDF)](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/chapters/washington-dc/documents/ebook_how_to_use_military_experience_to_qualify_for_the_pmp_exam.pdf) and [Vets2PM](https://vets2pm.com/pmp/) for help mapping your experience.
:::

### Cost

| Item | PMI Member | Non-Member |
|---|---|---|
| PMI membership | $164/year ($154 + $10 app) | — |
| PMP exam | $405 | $655 |
| Retake | $275 | $375 |
| 35 PDU training | $200-$1,500 | $200-$1,500 |
| **Total (first attempt)** | **~$769** | **~$2,155** |

### Funding
- [Army COOL Listing](https://www.cool.osd.mil/army/credential/index.html?cert=pmp2741)
- Credentialing Assistance eligible (up to $2,000)
- GI Bill reimburses up to $2,000 for the exam
- Third-party discounts: PPI2Pass (15% military), Master of Project Academy (50% military)

### Salary (2026)
- US median: $120,000
- 10+ years PMP: $173,000 median
- Defense contractor PM + Secret: $85,000-$115,000 (entry/mid)
- Senior defense PM + TS/SCI: $140,000-$160,000+

### DAU Alignment
- DAU courses count toward PMP 35-hour education requirement
- DAWIA PM credentials are now integrating PMP/PgMP requirements (2024-2025)
- Naval Postgraduate School offers PMI-accredited MS in Program Management where students earn PMP + DAWIA PM Practitioner credit simultaneously
- [DAU Program Management Training](https://www.dau.edu/cop/pm/page/Training)

## CAPM (Certified Associate in Project Management)

Entry-level alternative when you don't have 3+ years of PM experience.

- **Requirements**: 23 contact hours of PM education (no experience required)
- **Cost**: $225 PMI members / $300 non-members
- **Renewal**: Every 5 years; 15 PDUs
- Good stepping stone to PMP

## Agile & Scrum Certifications

| Cert | Issuer | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| PSM I | Scrum.org | $150 | No training required, open-book style, best value |
| CSM | Scrum Alliance | $800-$1,200 | Includes mandatory 2-day course |
| SAFe Scrum Master | Scaled Agile | $550-$750 | 2-day training required; $100/year renewal |
| PMI-ACP | PMI | $435-$495 | 21 hours Agile training + experience required |
| SAFe Agilist (SA) | Scaled Agile | ~$895 | Most common enterprise Agile cert |

:::tip[Best Value]
**PSM I ($150)** from Scrum.org requires no paid training, is well-respected, and never expires. Best entry point into Agile credentials.
:::

## Best MOS Fit

| Background | Recommended Path |
|---|---|
| 11-series (Infantry) | Leadership experience → PMP + Agile + clearance |
| 12-series (Engineer) | Construction PM experience → PMP directly |
| Logistics (88/92-series) | Supply chain PM → PMP + Agile |
| Any officer (O3+) | Direct PMP eligibility via command/staff experience |
| Acquisition workforce | DAU courses → PMP → DAWIA alignment |

## Certification Stacking

- **PMP + Agile/SAFe + Secret clearance** = defense PM ($100,000-$150,000)
- **PMP + DAWIA PM + TS/SCI** = senior acquisition PM ($140,000-$160,000+)
- **CAPM + Agile + entry** = junior PM role ($65,000-$85,000)
