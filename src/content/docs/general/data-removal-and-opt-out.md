# Data Removal and Opt-Out

1. 🛡️ About Data Removal Services
Data removal services are tools and platforms designed to help individuals safeguard their personal data and maintain online privacy. These services identify and removing sensitive personal information from data broker databases, people-search websites, and public directories.

They often assist in:

- Reducing spam emails and robocalls

- Protecting against phishing attacks and scams

- Minimizing personal information exposure

- Monitoring reappearances of your data (recurring removals)

- Simplifying the manual opt-out process

Some services operate on a subscription model, while others provide free DIY tools and email generators for data removal requests.
