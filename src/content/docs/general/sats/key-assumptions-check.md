# Key Assumptions Check

**What It Is:** A systematic review of the assumptions that underpin an analysis. Every judgment is based on assumptions, and this technique is used to explicitly list and question them.

**When to Use It:** At the beginning of a project to understand the foundational logic, or as a review of a draft analysis to ensure its premises are sound.

**Step-by-Step Guide:**
1. **STEP 1**: Gather a group, including outsiders, to brainstorm assumptions. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 185)
2. **STEP 2**: List assumptions on a whiteboard and critique them. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 185)
3. **STEP 3**: Categorize assumptions as supported, uncertain, or unsupported. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 186)
4. **STEP 4**: Refine the list and update based on group feedback. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 186)
