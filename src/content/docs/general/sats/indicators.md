# Indicators

**What It Is:** A diagnostic technique for identifying observable events or trends that can be monitored to track changes in a situation and provide early warning of anticipated events.

**When to Use It:** After developing a set of hypotheses or scenarios, to create a systematic way of monitoring which one is becoming more likely.

**Step-by-Step Guide:**
1. **STEP 1**: Brainstorm indicators for each scenario. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 328)
2. **STEP 2**: Refine the indicators, ensuring they are observable, valid, reliable, and stable. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 328)
