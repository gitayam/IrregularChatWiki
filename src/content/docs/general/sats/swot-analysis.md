# Strengths-Weaknesses-Opportunities-Threats (SWOT) Analysis



**What It Is:** A diagnostic technique used to identify the Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats related to a particular objective or organization.

**When to Use It:** For strategic planning, competitor analysis, or self-assessment.

**Step-by-Step Guide:**
1. **STEP 1**: Define the objective clearly. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 188)
2. **STEP 2**: List strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 188)
3. **STEP 3**: Identify strategies for exploiting strengths and opportunities, and mitigating weaknesses and threats.

**SWOT Analysis Template:**

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|------------|
| - Strength 1 | - Weakness 1 |
| - Strength 2 | - Weakness 2 |
| - Strength 3 | - Weakness 3 |

| Opportunities | Threats |
|---------------|---------|
| - Opportunity 1 | - Threat 1 |
| - Opportunity 2 | - Threat 2 |
| - Opportunity 3 | - Threat 3 |
