# Pros-Cons-Faults-and-Fixes

**What It Is:** A contrarian technique for critiquing a proposed decision or plan by systematically listing its pros, cons, the faults in the reasoning, and potential fixes.

**When to Use It:** To evaluate a proposed course of action or decision.

**Step-by-Step Guide:**
1. **STEP 1**: Define the decision clearly. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 277)
2. **STEP 2**: List pros and cons, and develop fixes for the cons. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 277)
3. **STEP 3**: Compare the pros and cons, and assess the risk associated with each. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 278)
