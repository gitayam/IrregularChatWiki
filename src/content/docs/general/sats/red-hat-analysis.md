# Red Hat Analysis

**What It Is:** A technique to avoid mirror-imaging by thinking about a problem from the perspective of an adversary or another party.

**When to Use It:** When analyzing the likely behavior or response of a competitor, adversary, or partner.

**Step-by-Step Guide:**
1. **STEP 1**: Gather analysts with knowledge of the target, environment, or decision-makers. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 323)
2. **STEP 2**: Use sticky notes to brainstorm without discussion. Focus on what the adversary would consider when acting. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 323)
3. **STEP 3**: Group and analyze ideas for common themes. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 323)
