# OPDW C100 Multi-Mission Platform

## OPDW C100 Multi-Mission Platform

### Overview
The OPDW C100 is a multi-mission platform equipped with military-grade payloads, advanced drivetrain, and Blue UAS approved avionics. It offers long-endurance organic ISR capabilities, edge computing, and a suite of autonomy features aimed at reducing cognitive burden and providing hands-free functionality. Its ruggedized IP54 design allows for easy transportation by folding into a compact form.


*[pdw-c100.jpeg]*

### Specifications

- **Folded / Unfolded Dimensions:** 24” x 15” x 8” / 64” x 65” x 11”

- **Weight:** 21.4 lbs (9.7 kg)

- **Max Payload Mass:** Up to 10 lbs (4.5 kg) with Long Endurance Pack (LEP) batteries

- **Long Endurance Battery:** 74 minutes with ISR payload

- **Range:** 6.2+ miles (10+ km)

- **Cruise Speed:** 22.3 mph (36 km/h)

- **Max Speed:** 40 mph (64.8 km/h)

- **Wind Limits:**

- ' 30 mph sustained, 40 mph gust (ISR configuration)

- ' 25 mph sustained, 35 mph gust (up to 5 lbs gross payload weight)

* 20 mph sustained, 30 mph gust (up to 10 lbs gross payload weight)

- **Night Operations:** Zero light emission mode with IR LEDs and strobe lights

- **Acoustic Signature:** 61dB @ 5m away / non-audible at 600 meters

- **Autonomy:** Visual Point Hold, Collision Prevention, Object Detection

- **Compute Core:** Modal AI’s VOXL1 Qualcomm 812 + optional Nvidia Xavier NX

- **Weatherization:** IP54

- **Supply Chain:** NDAA Compliant / Blue UAS

### Questions to Ask at Demonstration

1. **Visibility and Detection:**
#* How far away can the C100 be seen by enemies?
#* What are its IR and thermal signatures?
#* What is the acoustic signature in different operational modes?

1. **Data and Communication:**
#* What are the bandwidth requirements for usable FMV feeds from the sensor to the operator?
#* How is sensitive data managed on the drone? Is there local hosting or a store-and-forward mechanism that zeroizes upon impact?
#* Is telemetry parsed in a separate channel to facilitate multi-enclave / shareable classification products when working with partner forces?

1. **Battery and Power:**
#* Are the batteries proprietary? What is their lifespan and chemistry?
#* How does the drone perform in different environmental conditions such as winter, ice, rain, and high winds?
#* Is saltwater exposure an issue? How does it handle dust storms?

1. **Durability and Maintenance:**
#* What is the most commonly broken part, and should we stock spare parts in advance?
#* Is there a 24/7/365 manned hotline for support, reachable via public VOIP?

1. **Operational Use:**
#* Has the product been used in a theater of war, and by whom?
#* Can we get contact information for those users to discuss their experiences?
#* Can the drone be flown during the demonstration?

1. **Cost and Support:**
#* What is the price for the drone, ground control station (GCS), and enough batteries for continuous operation?
#* How is sensitive data secured on the drone, especially considering potential capture by adversaries?

### Notes from Prior Demonstrations

- The C100 is considered as good as it gets for current US DoD applications, particularly for GWOT operations.

- Not yet suitable for LSCO until frequency hopping radios and relay systems are integrated.

- Solid support for non-DoD CONUS operations.
