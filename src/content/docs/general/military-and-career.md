### SkillBridge

- Available during final 180 days of service
- Requires commander approval (O6 memo for some branches)
- Companies not on approved list can still participate with additional paperwork
- See [SkillBridge FAQ](https://skillbridge.osd.mil/faq.htm) and [Industry Employers](https://skillbridge.osd.mil/industry-employers.htm)

- [Awards Guide](/military/awards) - Writing guidelines and templates

### Key Points

1. Acronyms: Only use when appearing more than once
2. Numbers: Spell out 0-9, numerals for 10+
3. Ranks: Always abbreviate in achievement blocks
4. Explain: What they did AND how they did it

**Regulation**: [AR 600-8-22 Military Awards](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN30935-AR_600-8-22-000-WEB-1.pdf)
