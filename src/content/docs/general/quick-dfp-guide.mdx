## Privacy & Security Tools

### Pre-Browsing Checks

Confirm your configuration and VPN before proceeding:

1. [DNS / IP Leak Test](https://dnsleaktest.com/) - Confirm your location.
2. [Cover Your Tracks (EFF)](https://coveryourtracks.eff.org/) - Check browser fingerprinting.
3. [AdBlock Test](https://canyoublockit.com/extreme-test/) - Verify adblocking.

### Tools Comparison

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Password Managers" icon="password">
    See the [Password Manager Section](/general/guide-to-password-managers).

    - **Bitwarden** ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bitwarden-password-manager/id1137397744) | [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.x8bit.bitwarden) | [Desktop](https://bitwarden.com/download/))
    - **KeePass** ([iOS](https://keepassium.com/) | [Android](https://www.keepassdx.com/) | [Desktop](https://keepassxc.org/download/))

    :::danger[Avoid]
    **LastPass**: History of security breaches and proprietary nature.
    :::
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Authenticators (MFA)" icon="shieldCheck">
    - **2FAS** ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/2fas-auth/id1217793794) | [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.twofasapp))
    - **Aegis** ([Android](https://getaegis.app/)) - Open source and highly customizable.
    - **OTP Auth** ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/otp-auth/id659877384))
    - **Hardware**: [YubiKey](https://www.yubico.com/product/security-key-c-nfc-by-yubico/) is the gold standard.

    :::caution[Avoid]
    - **SMS**: Vulnerable to SIM-jacking.
    - **Authy**: Proprietary and requires a phone number.
    - **Google Authenticator**: Sync is not E2EE.
    :::
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="VPNs" icon="laptop">
    Full VPN Guide: [VPN Recommendation](/privacy/vpn-recommendation)

    1. **[MullvadVPN](https://mullvad.net/en/)**: No email, accepts cash, proven record.
    2. **[iVPN](https://ivpn.net)**: Transparent and no-logging.
    3. **[ProtonVPN](https://protonvpn.com)**: Secure Core and proven privacy.

    :::danger[Avoid]
    **ExpressVPN, CyberGhost, PIA, Zenmate**: All owned by KAPE Technologies (formerly an adware company).
    :::
  </TabItem>
