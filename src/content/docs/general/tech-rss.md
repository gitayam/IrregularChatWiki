# Tech RSS

## Tech RSS Recommendations

### Tech Reading

- [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/feed/) - Stay updated on technology news and trends.

- [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/rss/frontpage) - Comprehensive coverage of the latest in tech and culture.

- [Mashable](https://mashable.com/feeds/rss/all) - A mix of tech, entertainment, and social media news.

### Tech Listening

- [NPR Technology](https://www.npr.org/sections/technology/) - Explore technology through podcasts and discussions.

- [The Vergecast](https://www.theverge.com/tech-podcast) - A weekly podcast discussing the week in tech news.

### Tech Youtube Subscriptions

- [Brownlee](https://www.youtube.com/c/MarquesBrownlee|Marques) - In-depth reviews of the latest gadgets and technology trends.

- [Verge](https://www.youtube.com/c/TheVerge|The) - Engaging videos that highlight technology, culture, and the people who shape our world.

- [Therapy](https://www.youtube.com/c/UnboxTherapy|Unbox) - Hands-on unboxings of the latest tech products.
