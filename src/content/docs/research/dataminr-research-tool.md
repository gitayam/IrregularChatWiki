##### PAI User Form Notes

## The information provided under the *Army Account Information* is maintained by DAMO-CYF for account management and is not shared outside of the Army.

## Under *Minimum Profile Information*, the four essential elements required to create an account include Email, Common Profile, AOR, and type of application. These details can be updated anytime.

1. *Common Profile* refers to the category that best describes the role/mission, such as Leadership, Operations Center, Situational Awareness, Force Protection, or Information Environment.

1. *AOR* refers to the Area of Responsibility the user is interested in.

1. *Desktop/Mobile*: Choose between "Desktop Only" or "Desktop and Mobile" options. The mobile app is available for iOS and Android and is DISA-approved for personal and government-owned devices.

1. *Additional Profile Information* is optional and can be modified anytime via the dashboard. This includes geographic areas like country, state, and base/facility.
