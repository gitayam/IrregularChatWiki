# Force Field Analysis

**What It Is:** A diagnostic technique used to identify the forces that are either driving toward or restraining a particular goal or outcome.

**When to Use It:** When trying to understand the factors that are influencing a particular issue, or when developing a strategy to achieve a goal.

**Step-by-Step Guide:**
1. **STEP 1**: Define the problem or goal clearly. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 133)
2. **STEP 2**: Brainstorm the main factors influencing the issue. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 133)
3. **STEP 3**: Create two lists—one for supporting arguments (driving forces) and one for opposing ones (restraining forces). (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 133)
4. **STEP 4**: Assign values to the arguments to determine their strength. Calculate the total score to determine the dominant side. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 133)
