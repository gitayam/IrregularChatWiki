# Mind Maps

**What It Is:** A visual thinking tool that helps to structure information, helping you to better analyze, comprehend, synthesize, recall, and generate new ideas.

**When to Use It:** For brainstorming, note-taking, or planning out a complex topic.

**Step-by-Step Guide:**
1. **STEP 1**: Write the focal question at the center of the page. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 233)
2. **STEP 2**: Brainstorm possible explanations and group ideas into categories. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 233)
3. **STEP 3**: Expand the mind map by drawing connections between ideas. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 234)
