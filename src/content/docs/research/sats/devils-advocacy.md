# Devil's Advocacy

**What It Is:** A contrarian technique where an individual or a group is assigned the role of challenging a preferred hypothesis or plan by building the strongest possible case against it.

**When to Use It:** When a consensus view has formed around a particular explanation, especially on a high-stakes issue, to avoid groupthink.

**Step-by-Step Guide:**
1. **STEP 1**: Start with the project's goals, assumptions, and gaps. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 187)
2. **STEP 2**: Build a logical case against the proposed decision by focusing on potential pitfalls. (Beebe and Pherson, 2015, p. 187)
