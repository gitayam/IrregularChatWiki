# Research Datasets

## Datasets

### Finding Datasets

- [Google Data Set Search](https://datasetsearch.research.google.com/)

- [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/schhp?hl=en) Google Search Power for Academic Writing

- [JSTOR](https://www.jstor.org/) digital library of academic journals, books, and primary sources

- [Research Gate](https://www.researchgate.net/) Massive Database of Academic Journals

- [Google Dork](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3A.edu+%22free%22+%28%22research%22+or+%22dataset%22) for Academic Research Resources

- [Elicit](https://elicit.org) AI journal search.

- [Open Science Framework (OSF)](https://osf.io/) is a free and open-source platform that supports research and collaboration across the research life cycle.

- [Awesome Public Datasets](https://github.com/awesomedata/awesome-public-datasets) A curated list of over 40,000 public datasets across various topics.

- [Awesome Cyber Threat Datasets](https://github.com/hslatman/awesome-threat-intelligence) provide context, mechanisms, indicators, implications, and actionable advice about existing or emerging threats to assets that can inform decisions regarding the subject's response to that menace or hazard.

- [DataSet Tag](https://forum.irregularchat.com/tag/dataset) on IrregularChat Forum

- [r/datasets](https://www.reddit.com/r/datasets/)

- [r/Dissertation](https://www.reddit.com/r/Dissertation/)

- [r/AskAcademia](https://www.reddit.com/r/AskAcademia/)

- [r/GradSchool](https://www.reddit.com/r/GradSchool/)
