## Research Planning

Proper planning is crucial to ensure the success of any research project. This phase involves identifying the research question, formulating a hypothesis, and establishing clear objectives.

- [Research Preparation Guide](/research/research-preparation) - Full planning guide
- [Research Template](/research/research-template) - Standard template for research documentation
- [Research Tasks](/research/research-tasks) - Common research tasks and workflows

**Recommended Planning Tools:**
- [Goldilocker Tool](https://apps.lib.umich.edu/rq/) - Guided approach to refining research questions
- [ROOBRICK](https://www.roobrick.org/#/) - Collaborative COG Analysis with customizable weights
- [Mendeley](https://www.mendeley.com) - Organize and manage references
- [Draw.io](https://app.diagrams.net/) - Create mind maps (no login required)

## Processing Data

Once data has been collected, it must be processed and organized for analysis.

**Recommended Processing Tools:**
- [Cryptpad](https://cryptpad.irregularchat.com/sheet/) - E2EE spreadsheets, no login required
- [OSINTCombine](https://osintcombine.tools/) - Process and visualize CSV data
- [OpenRefine](https://openrefine.org) - Clean and transform messy data
- [RStudio](/general/rstudio) - Statistical computing and visualization
