## Claude for Government Overview

On February 17, 2026, Anthropic launched "Claude for Government," a specialized product tier designed for U.S. federal agencies, certified at FedRAMP High to manage sensitive unclassified data. This deployment is part of Anthropic's broader offerings and marks a significant advancement in AI capabilities for government use. The infrastructure for Claude for Government operates on Palantir's FedStar.

### Key Actors
- **Organizations**: Anthropic, Palantir, GSA
- **Locations**: IL5, IL6, United States
