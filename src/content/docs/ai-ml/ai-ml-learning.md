## Books

**Recommended Starting Point:**
- [Grand Strategy by John Lewis Gaddis](https://www.amazon.com/Grand-Strategy-John-Lewis-Gaddis/dp/1594203512) (Nick)

**Machine Learning Textbooks:**
- [Mathematics for Machine Learning](https://mml-book.com/) - This was one of the textbooks for the machine learning class at Carnegie Mellon. Highly recommended and still used as a resource. It covers all the math principles with Python exercises. (Matt)

**For Non-Technical Leaders:**
Book recommendation for non-technical leaders who are struggling to understand how to implement AI/DL/ML strategies and practices into the organization. It's not super technical, but as more leaders take their organizational frameworks from business it's a good way for them to comprehend the underlying importance of a structured framework for the holistic implementation of these algorithms. (Sam)

**Additional Reading:**
- [Private Empire: ExxonMobil and American Power](https://www.amazon.com/Private-Empire-ExxonMobil-American-Power/dp/0143123548)
- [Bitter Fruit: The Story of the American Coup in Guatemala](https://www.amazon.com/Bitter-Fruit-American-Guatemala-Expanded/dp/067401930X)
- Theodore Shackley's "The Third Option" - He wrote about the need to provide alternate options but through the lens of the Cold War. Basically, counter Soviet sneakiness with our own. (Nick)
