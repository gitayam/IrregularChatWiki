# Cyber Decks & Custom Hardware

## Portable & Hackable Devices
The community frequently explores custom-built, cyberpunk-inspired portable devices for radio control, penetration testing, and general utility.

### Carbon Computers
Specializes in cutting-edge cyber solutions and custom-built devices.
- **PI FLUX**: A portable, modular computing platform.
- **Cybert**: Handheld devices supporting multiple OSs like Kali Linux, BlackArch, and Windows.
- **Cyber uConsole**: Variations of handheld tech designed for field use.

### Handheld Field Gear
For mobile radio operations (e.g., CAT control), users often weigh the benefits of specialized handhelds against cheap, ruggedized laptops. Key considerations include:
- **Portability**: Specialized handhelds offer superior form factors for field use.
- **Power Consumption**: ARM-based SBCs (like Raspberry Pi) are often more efficient for long-duration mobile operations.
- **Interface**: Built-in displays and keyboards in a unified chassis reduce the number of components to manage in the field.
