# Cybersecurity-university-courses

1. 11 FREE University Cybersecurity Courses

1. [Introduction to Cyber Attacks at NYU](https://www.classcentral.com/course/intro-cyber-attacks-9667) — Covers types of cyber attacks, defense strategies, and current trends.

1. [Cryptography I at Stanford](https://www.coursera.org/learn/crypto) — Learn fundamental principles of modern cryptographic systems and security protocols.

1. [Hardware Security at University of Maryland](https://www.coursera.org/learn/hardware-security) — Focuses on securing hardware components, including cryptographic hardware design.

1. [Network and Security Foundations at Western Governors University](https://www.edx.org/course/network-security-foundations) — An introduction to network security concepts and communication security.

1. [Internet History, Technology, and Security at University of Michigan](https://online.umich.edu/courses/internet-history-technology-and-security/) — Explores the history, development, and security of the internet.

1. [Network and Computer Security at MIT](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-857-network-and-computer-security-spring-2014/) — Detailed course on security protocols and network security principles.

1. [Computer Networks at MIT](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-829-computer-networks-fall-2002/) — Focuses on network architecture, protocols, and security.

1. [System Issues in Cloud Computing Specialization at Georgia Tech](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/cloud) — Learn about the challenges and solutions related to cloud computing security.

1. [Cybersecurity Management at Charles Sturt University](https://itmasters.edu.au/free-short-course-cybersecurity-management/) — Covers principles and practices for managing cybersecurity within an organization.

1. [Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) at Charles Sturt University](https://itmasters.edu.au/free-short-course-cissp-updated/) — Prepares for CISSP certification, covering core security concepts.

1. [Pen Testing at Charles Sturt University](https://itmasters.edu.au/free-short-course-pen-testing/) — Introduction to penetration testing and ethical hacking methodologies.
