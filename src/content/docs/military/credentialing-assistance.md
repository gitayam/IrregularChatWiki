## What is Credentialing Assistance (CA)?

Credentialing Assistance (CA) is an Army program that funds professional certifications and credentials aligned with career goals.

:::danger[Major Changes — December 2024]
The Army significantly reduced CA funding effective FY2025:

| Detail | Old | New (FY2025+) |
|---|---|---|
| Annual cap | $4,000 | **$2,000** |
| Credentials per year | Multiple | **1 per year** |
| Lifetime limit | None | **3 per decade (6 per career)** |
| Officers eligible? | Yes | **No** — commissioned and warrant officers excluded |
| Aviation credentials | $4,000 | **$1,000 max** |

Army TA was increased to partially offset the CA cut. See [Certification Funding Guide](/military/certification-funding) for all available programs.

Sources: [Army.mil announcement](https://www.army.mil/article/281853/army_approves_tuition_assistance_increase_adjusts_credentialing_program), [Military.com](https://www.military.com/daily-news/2024/12/10/army-cuts-popular-education-benefit-half-bars-officers-costs-soar)
:::

:::tip[Other Branches]
Navy, Air Force, and Marine Corps do not have a standalone CA program. Use [COOL voucher programs](https://www.cool.osd.mil/) instead. See the full [Certification Funding Guide](/military/certification-funding).
:::

## Using CA for SANS Training

### What is SANS?

SANS (SysAdmin, Audit, Network, and Security) Institute provides advanced training and certifications in cybersecurity. Check the [SANS Roadmap](https://www.sans.org/cyber-security-skills-roadmap/) for career progression.

**Recommended Certification**: [SEC504: Hacker Tools, Techniques, and Incident Handling — GCIH](https://www.sans.org/cyber-security-courses/hacker-techniques-incident-handling/)

### Process

1. **Create a SANS Account** at [sans.org/account/create](https://www.sans.org/account/create/) using your .mil email

2. **Order On-Demand Training** at [sans.org/ondemand](https://www.sans.org/ondemand/)
   - Select your desired course and proceed to checkout
   - Select "Pay by Phone or Multiple Credit Cards"
   - Note your order number

3. **Request CA discount** — email SANS requesting the price be reduced to match your CA cap ($2,000):

   > I request to have the discount down to $2,000 (the total annual amount for CA) for the following training: [your order details]

4. **Complete ArmyIgnitED submission** after receiving the updated SANS quote:
   - Log in to [ArmyIgnitED](https://www.armyignited.com/app/)
   - [Request Credentialing Assistance](https://www.armyignited.com/app/student/credential-paths/request)
   - Select "Other for MOS"
   - Search for Pathway: GIAC Certified Incident Handler (GCIH) or your chosen cert
   - Upload the quote received from SANS

:::caution[Plan Ahead]
Submit CA requests through ArmyIgnitED **90 days before** your course/exam start date. Requests open 90 days out.
:::
