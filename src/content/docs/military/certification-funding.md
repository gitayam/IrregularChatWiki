Every funding pathway available to active duty, veterans, Guard/Reserve, and military spouses for professional certifications.

:::danger[Army CA Cut in Half — December 2024]
Army Credentialing Assistance was reduced from **$4,000 to $2,000/year**. Officers are now excluded entirely. See [Credentialing Assistance](/military/credentialing-assistance) for updated details.
:::

## Active Duty Programs

### Credentialing Assistance (CA) — Army Only

| Detail | Current (FY2025+) |
|---|---|
| Annual cap | **$2,000** (was $4,000) |
| Credentials per year | 1 |
| Lifetime limit | 3 per decade, 6 per career |
| Officers eligible? | **No** — commissioned and warrant officers excluded |
| Aviation credentials | $1,000 max |

- **Process**: Submit through [ArmyIgnitED](https://www.armyignited.army.mil/app/) 90 days before exam/course
- **Eligible credentials**: Look up by MOS at [Army COOL](https://www.cool.osd.mil/army/costs_and_funding/index.html)
- Recertification costs count against $2,000 cap but not the 3-per-decade credential limit

:::tip[Other Branches]
Navy, Air Force, and Marine Corps do not have a standalone CA program. Their credentialing support runs through COOL voucher programs (see below).
:::

### Tuition Assistance (TA)

| Branch | Per Credit Hour | Annual Cap | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Army | $250 | $4,500 | Increased to offset CA cut |
| Navy | $250 | $3,000 (12 credits max) | |
| Air Force | $250 | $4,500 (18 SH max) | |
| Marine Corps | $250 | $4,500 (18 SH max) | |
| Guard/Reserve | $250 | $4,500 (16 SH max) | Must be on Title 10/32 orders or drill status |

**Can TA pay for cert prep?** Only if the course is part of an approved academic degree or certificate program at an accredited institution. Standalone exam prep is NOT TA-eligible.

**Portals:**
- Army: [ArmyIgnitED](https://www.armyignited.army.mil/)
- Navy: [MyNavy Education](https://www.navycollege.navy.mil/sailors/tuition-assistance-ncpace.htm)
- Air Force: AF Portal (myFSS) via base education center
- Marine Corps: MarineNet / base education center

### SOF Education Program (SEP)

- **Amount**: Up to $10,000/year
- **Purpose**: Individual knowledge gaps in special operations roles
- **Separate from**: TA and CA (additional funding — does not duplicate existing programs)
- **Requirements**: SF-182 submission, no planned separation within 18 months
- **Approval chain**: Company Commander → Battalion Commander → SWCS signatures
- **Guide**: [SEP Funds Guide](https://u.pcloud.link/publink/show?code=XZ0frk0ZchOJr4ELEVkiFyB7Ic45CmhSpaEk)

:::tip[SEP is Additional Money]
SEP is separate from both TA and CA. If you're SOF, this is up to $10,000/year on top of your other education benefits. Contact the Education Office for details.
:::

### COOL Programs (Credentialing Opportunities On-Line)

Central portal for all branches: [cool.osd.mil](https://www.cool.osd.mil/)

**Army COOL** — [cool.osd.mil/army](https://www.cool.osd.mil/army/)
- Funds through CA program ($2,000/year for enlisted)
- Look up eligible credentials by MOS

**Navy COOL** — [cool.osd.mil/usn](https://www.cool.osd.mil/usn/)
- Check portal for current FY funding status (funding has been inconsistent)
- Authorization must happen BEFORE scheduling the exam — after-the-fact requests are denied
- Contact: (850) 473-5996

**Marine Corps COOL** — [cool.osd.mil/usmc](https://www.cool.osd.mil/usmc/)
- Covers exam fees, recertification, maintenance fees
- Does NOT fund training, study materials, or exam prep

**Air Force COOL** — [cool.osd.mil/usaf](https://www.cool.osd.mil/usaf/)

### Unit/Command Training Funds (SF-182)

- SF-182 is the standard form for commander-discretionary training
- No fixed dollar limit — varies by unit budget and mission
- Route through: supervisor → unit training officer (S3/G3) → budget officer → approval authority
- Most units burn funds in Q3-Q4 (Apr-Sep) — submit early in the fiscal year

## Veteran & Transition Programs

### GI Bill Certification Test Reimbursement (Chapter 33)

The VA reimburses up to **$2,000 per exam** for qualifying licensing and certification tests.

**Process:**
1. Schedule and pay for your exam out of pocket
2. Submit [VA Form 22-0803](https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-22-0803-ARE.pdf) within **one year** of the test date
3. Apply: [VA Licensing and Certification Tests](https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/how-to-use-benefits/licensing-and-certification-tests/)

:::caution[Important Limitations]
- **No housing allowance** for standalone test reimbursements (MHA only paid during school enrollment)
- Entitlement is charged proportionally (~1 month per $2,578.64 reimbursed in FY2026)
- **One-year deadline** to submit reimbursement is firm
- Covers exam fees only — not study materials, training, or prep courses
:::

### VET TEC

:::caution[Program in Transition]
VA stopped accepting applications April 1, 2024. Re-authorized January 2025 through September 2027, but as of early 2025 the VA has NOT yet approved training providers. Check current status at the [VET TEC page](https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/how-to-use-benefits/vettec-high-tech-program/).
:::

When operational:
- Covers accelerated tech training (6-28 weeks: bootcamps, cybersecurity, cloud)
- Does **NOT** use GI Bill entitlement
- Provides housing stipend during training
- Apply using VA Form 22-0994

### VR&E / Chapter 31 (Veteran Readiness & Employment)

For veterans with **service-connected disabilities** (10%+ rating).

- Can fund: tuition, certifications, training, equipment
- Apply: [VA VR&E](https://www.va.gov/careers-employment/vocational-rehabilitation/)
- VA assigns a Vocational Rehabilitation Counselor (VRC) who develops an Individualized Employment Plan
- If discharged after January 1, 2013: no time limit on eligibility

:::tip[Advocate for Yourself]
VR&E approval is counselor-dependent. A proactive VRC can fund a broad range of certifications. Know your rights and be specific about career goals.
:::

### DoD SkillBridge

Use your **last 180 days** of active duty for civilian training while still receiving military pay and benefits.

**Requirements:** 180+ continuous days active service, command approval, all TAP requirements complete. Start the process 9-12 months before separation.

Popular programs include Microsoft MSSA, Amazon Technical Apprenticeship, Salesforce Military Fellows, and Hiring Our Heroes Fellowship.

**Full SkillBridge directory**: See the [Leaving Service](/military/leaving-service) page for the complete community-sourced program list.

**Find all programs:** [skillbridge.osd.mil](https://skillbridge.osd.mil/)

## Free Corporate Programs

These cost $0 and don't use any military benefits.

### Onward to Opportunity (O2O)

- **Provider**: Syracuse University / IVMF
- **Cost**: Free — 40+ professional certifications
- **Eligible**: Active duty (180 days out), veterans, spouses, Guard/Reserve
- **Apply**: [ivmf.syracuse.edu/programs/career-training](https://ivmf.syracuse.edu/programs/career-training/about-o2o/)

### Microsoft MSSA

- **Cost**: Free (fully funded by Microsoft)
- **Tracks**: Cybersecurity Ops, Cloud App Dev, Server & Cloud Admin
- **Eligible**: Transitioning SM (within 180 days), veterans (honorable discharge), military spouses
- **URL**: [military.microsoft.com/mssa](https://military.microsoft.com/mssa/)
- **Note**: CSO track requires active CompTIA Security+ as prerequisite

### AWS Programs

- [AWS re/Start](https://aws.amazon.com/training/restart/) — 12-week full-time cloud training, free, with job placement
- [AWS Educate](https://aws.amazon.com/education/awseducate/) — free for veterans and military spouses
- [AWS Certification GI Bill Reimbursement](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/veterans-reimbursement/) — submit VA Form 22-0803
- [Cloud Veterans](https://www.cloudveterans.org/) — nonprofit supporting military cloud careers

### Google Career Certificates

- [Grow with Google — Military](https://grow.google/expanding-opportunity/military-community/) — free through military partnerships
- Tracks: Data Analytics, IT Support, Project Management, UX Design, Cybersecurity
- [SVA/Coursera Access](https://studentveterans.org/info-google-certificates/) — free through Student Veterans of America
- [Google Cloud Launchpad for Veterans](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/training-certifications/google-cloud-launchpad-veterans-to-cloud-careers)

### Salesforce Military

- [Trailhead Military](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/vetforce) — free training + free exam voucher
- Eligible: active duty, Reserve, Guard, veterans, spouses
- 2025 relaunch includes AI/Salesforce integration training

### Cisco NetAcad

- [Cisco Military Programs](https://www.netacad.com/region/north-america/military) — free for military, veterans, and families
- CyberVetsUSA: free CCNA Security or CCNA Cyber Ops track
- [Military Credentialing Assistance](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/learn/training-certifications/training/military-credentialing.html)

## Military Spouse Programs

### MyCAA (My Career Advancement Account)

- **Amount**: Up to $4,000 total ($2,000/year max)
- **Eligible**: Spouses of active duty E-1 to E-9, W-1 to W-3, O-1 to O-3
- Also eligible: spouses of KIA, POW, MIA, severely wounded; Guard/Reserve spouses on Title 10 orders
- **Covers**: Licenses, certificates, certifications, associate degrees at approved institutions
- **Portal**: [mycaa.militaryonesource.mil](https://mycaa.militaryonesource.mil/mycaa/)
- **Support**: 800-342-9647

:::caution[MyCAA Timing]
Must request financial assistance **within 30 days of course start date**. Cannot be requested retroactively. Coordinate with your school's financial aid office early.
:::

### Spouse Scholarships

- [NMFA Scholarships](https://www.militaryfamily.org/) — for spouses of AD, Reserve, Guard, retirees, survivors
- [Blue Star Families](https://bluestarfam.org/) — career development programs
- [Hiring Our Heroes Career Forward](https://www.hiringourheroes.org/career-services/education-networking/career-forward/) — Google Career Certs for spouses

## Stacking Programs

Programs that can be combined for maximum benefit:

| Scenario | Combination |
|---|---|
| Active duty Army enlisted | CA ($2,000) + COOL voucher + TA (for prep courses at school) |
| Transitioning SM | SkillBridge + O2O + MSSA (if timing works) |
| Veteran | GI Bill test reimbursement ($2,000/exam) + VR&E (if disability) |
| Veteran (tech) | VET TEC (when restored) — uses **no** GI Bill entitlement |
| Military spouse | MyCAA ($4,000) + Google Career Certs (free) + Salesforce Military (free) |
| Guard/Reserve | Federal TA + State TA (many states) + COOL (when funded) |

:::danger[Cannot Stack]
- SkillBridge and other DoD-funded training cannot run simultaneously
- GI Bill and VR&E cannot be used at the same time for the same training
:::
