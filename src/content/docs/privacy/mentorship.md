Working notepad found [here on cryptpad](https://cryptpad.irregularchat.com/pad/#/2/pad/edit/fBn5TxJJt0XQ1cWuY3A2hgdP/) ### Problem Statement &gt; A critical challenge emerges in the Irregular Chat community: effectively scaling mentorship to meet the high demand for specialized guidance against a backdrop of limited mentor availability. Existing frameworks, spanning official military to community-based, show their age and lack the necessary flexibility to meet members’ diverse goals, from technical skill development to career advancement. This gap stifles individual growth potential and prevents the full application of collective expertise. Innovating an adaptive, scalable mentorship model becomes essential, aiming to forge meaningful connections between mentors and mentees tailored to specific needs and aspirations.


### Just Cause
> to perpetually innovate mentorship within the Irregular Chat community, ensuring it remains adaptive and scalable to meet ever-evolving needs. We aim to foster an environment where every member can access personalized mentorship, unlocking individual potential and collective expertise.
